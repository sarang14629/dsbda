# 1. What is Descriptive Statistics?

Definition: Descriptive Statistics is a method used to collect, organize, summarize, and present data in a meaningful way.

Example:

Suppose we have marks of 5 students:

`50, 60, 70, 80, 90`

We can calculate:

- Mean = 70
- Median = 70
- Mode = No repeated value
- Minimum = 50
- Maximum = 90

Purpose: To understand large amounts of data easily.

# 2. Important concepts for viva

## A. Measures of Central Tendency

These measures tell us the central or typical value of a dataset.

1\. Mean (Average)

Sum of all values divided by the total number of values.

\\[ Mean=\frac{\sum x}{n} \\]

Example: 10, 20, 30

Mean = (10 + 20 + 30) / 3 = 20

2\. Median (Middle Value)

The middle value after arranging data in ascending order.

Example: 10, 20, 30

Median = 20

3\. Mode (Most Frequent Value)

The value that occurs most frequently.

Example: 10, 20, 20, 30

Mode = 20

## B. Measures of Variability

These tell us how spread out the data is.

| Measure            | Meaning                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Range              | Maximum − Minimum                   |
| Variance           | Average squared deviation from mean |
| Standard Deviation | Measures spread around mean         |
| IQR                | Q3 − Q1                             |

Important formulas:

Range

\\[ Range=Maximum-Minimum \\]

Variance

\\[ \sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x-\mu)^2}{N} \\]

Standard Deviation

\\[ \sigma=\sqrt{Variance} \\]

IQR

\\[ IQR=Q3-Q1 \\]
